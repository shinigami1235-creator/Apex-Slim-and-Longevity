How cold removes fat cells

Fat cells freeze at a higher temperature than skin, nerves and muscle, and CoolSculpting works in that gap.

Cooling

The applicator draws the area in and cools it for about 35 minutes.

Crystals form

Fat cells crystallise at a temperature the skin above them can take, and the damaged cells start to break down.

Clearing

Over 1 to 3 months the body clears the dead fat cells through the lymph system.
