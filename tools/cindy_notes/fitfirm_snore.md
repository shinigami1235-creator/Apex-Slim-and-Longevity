SNORELASE (snoring laser)
- Technology: laser that tightens muscles in the mouth, opens the airway, reduces vibration and snoring sound (poster 11). Poster 13 cites an erbium:YAG (2940 nm) study; the posters do not state this is the clinic's own device.
- Treated sites: soft palate, inner cheek, base of the tongue (poster 13).
