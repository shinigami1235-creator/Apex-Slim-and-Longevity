Your first visit

Every plan at Apex Slim starts with a body composition scan and a doctor consultation since the scan shows how much of your weight is fat, muscle and water.

First visit Included in every plan

  1. Body composition scanFat mass, muscle mass and body water, measured on the day.
  2. Doctor consultationThe doctor reads the scan with you and goes over your health history.
  3. Plan with a nutritionistMeals and treatment sessions planned around your week.

Longevity

Longevity at Apex Slim covers recovery, energy and sleep, and each treatment can run alongside a slimming plan.

Iv

IV Therapy

Vitamins, minerals and fluids given through a drip in 30 to 60 minutes, mixed by the doctor for your goal.

 Hb

HBOT

You breathe pure oxygen in a pressurised chamber for 60 to 90 minutes, which raises the oxygen carried in your blood.

 Sl

Snorelase

A laser tightens the soft palate to reduce snoring over 3 short sessions with no cuts and no anaesthetic.
