Your first visit

Every plan at Apex Slim starts with a body composition scan and a doctor consultation since the scan shows how much of your weight is fat, muscle and water.

First visit Included in every plan

  1. Body composition scanFat mass, muscle mass and body water, measured on the day.
  2. Doctor consultationThe doctor reads the scan with you and goes over your health history.
  3. Plan with a nutritionistMeals and treatment sessions planned around your week.

Longevity

Longevity at Apex Slim covers recovery, energy and sleep, and each treatment can run alongside a slimming plan.

Iv

IV Therapy

Vitamins, minerals and fluids given through a drip in 30 to 60 minutes, mixed by the doctor for your goal.

 Hb

HBOT

You breathe pure oxygen in a pressurised chamber for 60 minutes, which raises the oxygen carried in your blood.

 Sl

Snorelase

A laser tightens the soft palate to reduce snoring over 3 to 6 sessions of about 30 minutes with no cuts and no anaesthetic.

Lab Notes

How each treatment works, how long it takes and what to expect after.

Wk5 min read

Why weight comes back after a diet

Dieting changes your hunger hormones, and in one study they were still changed a year later.

 Cs5 min read

How cold removes fat cells

Fat cells freeze at a warmer temperature than skin, so cooling the skin can kill the fat under it.

 En4 min read

What Emsculpt Neo does in 30 minutes

Heat and magnetic pulses work on the fat and the muscle at the same time.

 Ff4 min read

How heat smooths cellulite

Radiofrequency warms the layer under the skin and makes it build new collagen.

 Iv5 min read

What a drip can do that a tablet can't

Vitamin C by drip reaches levels in the blood that tablets can't.

 Hb4 min read

How pressure puts more oxygen in your blood

Breathing pure oxygen under pressure pushes extra oxygen into the plasma.

 Sl4 min read

Why you snore and how a laser helps

Loose tissue at the back of your throat flaps as you breathe, and heat tightens it.
