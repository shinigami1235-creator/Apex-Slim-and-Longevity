1 Week 1 Kilo

If you have lost weight before and watched it come back, willpower was probably not the problem. When you eat less, your body raises the hormone that makes you hungry and lowers the ones that make you feel full, and it keeps doing this long after the diet ends.

In a study in the New England Journal of Medicine, 50 people lost an average of 13.5 kg on a 10-week diet. A year later their hunger hormones had still not gone back to normal and they felt hungrier than before they started. 1 Week 1 Kilo is a weight-loss plan run by a doctor who sees you every week and treats that hunger as part of the problem.

Why the weight comes back

Fat tissue makes a hormone called leptin that tells your brain how much energy you have stored. When you diet and lose fat, leptin drops and your brain reads the drop as a food shortage. Your stomach then releases more ghrelin, the hormone that makes you hungry, while your gut releases less of the hormones that make you feel full after a meal.

The NEJM study measured these hormones in the same people for more than a year. Leptin and the fullness hormones were still low and ghrelin was still high at the 62-week check, more than a year after the diet started.

Eating less on your own means fighting this hunger every day, so the plan tracks your hunger as closely as your weight and adjusts every week.

Why Thailand uses a lower BMI scale

A WHO expert panel found that Asian people carry more body fat and a higher risk of diabetes and heart disease than Europeans at the same BMI. Thailand counts a BMI of 23 as overweight and 25 as obese for that reason, where many Western charts use 25 and 30.

Your waist tells the doctor where the fat sits. A waist of 90 cm or more for men or 80 cm or more for women points to fat around the organs, and so does a waist larger than half your height. You can check both numbers in the Wellness Lab before your visit.

How the plan works

  1. Your first visit is a consultation with the doctor, who goes through your health and weight history with your scan results.
  2. You set a goal together. The first target is usually 5 to 10 percent of your body weight since that amount already improves blood pressure and blood sugar.
  3. If the doctor prescribes medicine to control hunger, it starts at the lowest dose and goes up step by step to keep side effects down.
  4. You get an eating plan you can follow at home with a daily target for water.
  5. You come back every week to weigh in and talk through hunger, meals and side effects, and the doctor adjusts the plan.
  6. Exercise is added as you go, building up to 150 to 300 minutes a week.

The weekly visit

Each visit starts with a weigh-in and a waist measurement. The doctor then asks how hungry you have been, what you ate and how you felt, and changes one or two things for the coming week. A body composition scan at set points shows how much of the change is fat and how much is water or muscle.

What to bring to your first visit

  • A list of the medicines and supplements you take
  • Any blood test results from the past year
  • Your weight history, including diets you have tried and how much came back

Who should not join

The doctor will not start the plan, or will leave medicine out of it, if you:

  • are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy
  • have a family history of medullary thyroid cancer or a hormone gland tumour
  • have had pancreatitis
  • have severe stomach, bowel, liver or kidney disease
  • have severe depression

Side effects

If the plan includes medicine, the common side effects are nausea, bloating, vomiting, constipation and diarrhoea. They are most likely in the first weeks and after a dose goes up, and they usually ease as the body adjusts. Losing weight quickly also raises the chance of gallstones, so tell the doctor straight away about strong pain in the upper right of your stomach.

Questions

Will I lose 1 kilo every week?

The name is the pace the plan aims for. Weight often starts to come off in 2 to 4 weeks, and the doctor keeps the pace at what is safe for your body.

Do I have to give up foods I like?

No food is banned. The eating plan works out portions you can keep up for months since a plan you drop after two weeks does nothing.

Can I combine it with CoolSculpting?

Yes. CoolSculpting removes fat from one area without changing your weight much, so the doctor often starts with 1 Week 1 Kilo and treats a stubborn area once you are near your goal.

What happens when the plan ends?

The doctor plans the end with you. The eating and exercise habits you build at the weekly visits are what keep the weight off afterwards.
