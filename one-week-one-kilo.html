1 Week 1 Kilo

Most people who lose weight on a diet put some of it back on. A 2011 study in the New England Journal of Medicine found that after a diet, the body makes more ghrelin, the hormone that makes you hungry, and less of the hormones that tell you you're full.

The 50 people in that study lost an average of 13.5 kg over 10 weeks. A year later their hormones still hadn't gone back to normal and they still felt hungrier than before they started. 1 Week 1 Kilo is our weight-loss program for people who regain weight after dieting. You see the doctor every week, and the plan is adjusted around your hunger as well as your weight.

What happens to your hunger after a diet

Leptin is a hormone your fat tissue makes. It tells your brain how much energy you have stored. When you lose fat, your leptin level drops and your brain reacts as if food were running short. Your stomach then makes more ghrelin and your gut makes less of the hormones that make you feel full after eating.

In the NEJM study these changes were still there at 62 weeks, more than a year after the diet started. Since the changes last, eating less on your own gets harder the longer you keep it up.

The Asian BMI scale

A WHO expert panel found in 2004 that Asian people carry more body fat than Europeans at the same BMI and get diabetes and heart disease at a lower BMI. Since Asian people carry more fat at the same BMI, Thailand uses 23 as the cut-off for overweight and 25 for obese, while many Western charts use 25 and 30.

The doctor also measures your waist since it shows where the fat is. A waist of 90 cm or more in men or 80 cm or more in women means more fat around the organs, and so does a waist more than half your height. You can check your BMI and waist in the Wellness Lab before you come in.

How the program works

  1. At the first visit the doctor goes through your health, your weight history and your body composition scan.
  2. You agree on a goal with the doctor. The first goal is usually 5 to 10 percent of your body weight since losing that much already improves blood pressure and blood sugar.
  3. If the doctor prescribes medicine to control your hunger, you start on the lowest dose. The dose goes up slowly to keep side effects down.
  4. You get an eating plan to follow at home and a target for how much water to drink each day.
  5. You come back every week to weigh in and talk about your hunger and any side effects. The doctor adjusts the plan from there.
  6. Exercise is added over time until you reach 150 to 300 minutes a week.

Weekly visits

Every visit starts with your weight and waist. The doctor asks how hungry you've been and what you ate, then changes one or two things for the next week. You also get a body composition scan at set points in the program to see how much of what you lost is fat and how much is water or muscle.

Who can't join

The doctor won't start the program, or will leave the medicine out, if you:

  • are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy
  • have a family history of medullary thyroid cancer or a hormone gland tumour
  • have had pancreatitis
  • have severe stomach, bowel, liver or kidney disease
  • have severe depression

Side effects

The medicine can cause nausea, bloating, vomiting, constipation or diarrhoea, most often in the first few weeks and after the dose goes up. They usually get better as your body gets used to it. Losing weight fast can also cause gallstones. Tell the doctor straight away if you get strong pain in the upper right part of your stomach.

What to bring to your first visit

  • the medicines and supplements you take
  • blood test results from the past year if you have them
  • your weight history, including any diets you've tried and how much weight came back

Questions

Will I lose 1 kilo every week?

That's the pace the program aims for. Weight often starts to drop in 2 to 4 weeks, and the doctor keeps the pace at what's safe for you.

Do I have to give up foods I like?

No food is banned. The eating plan sets portions you can keep up for months.

Can I do CoolSculpting too?

Yes. CoolSculpting removes fat from one area but barely changes your weight, so the doctor often starts you on 1 Week 1 Kilo and treats the stubborn area once you're close to your goal.

What happens when the program ends?

The doctor plans the end of the program with you. Keep up the eating and exercise habits from the weekly visits since your hunger hormones can stay changed for more than a year.
