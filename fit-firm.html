FIT FIRM

Loose skin on the thighs and a soft belly often stay after the weight has gone, and exercise alone is slow to change them. FIT FIRM is the Apex Slim program for firming these areas without needles or downtime.

It combines two kinds of energy. Radiofrequency (RF) heats the deeper layer of the skin, which makes the body build new collagen over the following months, and electromagnetic stimulation makes the muscle underneath contract over and over the way it does in training.

How heat firms skin

Collagen is the protein that keeps skin firm, and the body makes less of it as you age. When RF heats the deeper layer of the skin to a controlled temperature, the collagen fibres already there tighten and the heat switches on the cells that make new collagen. The new collagen forms over weeks to months, so the skin keeps firming after the sessions end.

Why add muscle stimulation

A firm muscle under the skin makes an area look tighter. Electromagnetic stimulation makes the muscle contract in strong, repeated pulses while you lie still, and a muscle worked this way over a course of sessions gets firmer.

A 2022 study gave 38 people 3 sessions of combined RF and muscle stimulation. Fat thickness and body measurements were smaller after treatment, the participants were satisfied with the result and there were no serious side effects. Results differ between devices, and the doctor shows you the device Apex uses at your consult.

What to expect after a session

Straight after a session the area can look tighter and feel warm. Part of that early change is warmth and fluid that settles within a day or two, and the lasting change comes from new collagen and firmer muscle over the following weeks.

You can go back to work straight away. The skin can stay pink for a few hours and the muscles can feel worked, the same as after exercise.

Your course step by step

  1. At your consult the doctor pinches and measures the area, checks how loose the skin is and takes photos to compare later.
  2. The doctor sets the number of sessions and the gap between them from what the area needs.
  3. At each session a gel or pad goes on the skin and the applicator is moved or strapped over the area while it warms and the muscle contracts.
  4. You come back after the course for measurements and photos, which show the change better than the mirror does since it builds slowly.

Who it suits

FIT FIRM suits people with mild looseness or softness in the belly, thighs or upper arms, often after weight loss or pregnancy. Very loose skin after a large weight loss may need surgery to remove, and the doctor tells you at the consult if FIT FIRM cannot give you the change you want.

It is not used during pregnancy, for people with a pacemaker or other electronic implant, over metal implants, or on skin with an infection or open wound.

Questions

How is FIT FIRM different from Emsculpt Neo?

Both pair heat with muscle stimulation. Emsculpt Neo is built to remove fat and build muscle, while FIT FIRM works on firming the skin and toning the muscle under it.

How many sessions will I need?

The doctor sets the number at your consult after looking at the area. Firming builds over a course of sessions since new collagen takes weeks to form.

Does it hurt?

It feels warm with strong muscle contractions. The nurse turns the settings down if it gets too hot.

When will I see the result?

The skin keeps firming for weeks after the last session since new collagen takes time to form. The photos at your review show the change most clearly.

Can men do it?

Yes. FIT FIRM works the same way for men, most often on the belly and the upper arms.

Can I do it after CoolSculpting?

Yes. FIT FIRM can firm the skin over an area after CoolSculpting has thinned the fat, and the doctor plans the timing.
