FIT FIRM

FIT FIRM is our program for cellulite and loose skin. Cellulite is the dimpled, orange-peel skin that shows most on the thighs, hips and buttocks, and it often stays after losing weight or having a baby even when the rest of you has slimmed down.

FIT FIRM uses a radiofrequency (RF) handpiece at 448 kHz to warm the layer under your skin. The heat helps break down stored fat there and makes your skin build new collagen and elastin over the next few months. Each session takes about 20 minutes with no needles and no downtime.

What causes cellulite

Under the skin, fat sits in small pockets held in place by bands of collagen that run down to the muscle. When the pockets fill up, the fat pushes up between the bands while the bands hold the skin down, and the surface looks dimpled. Women get cellulite far more often than men since their bands run straight up toward the skin.

Diet and exercise help by shrinking the fat in the pockets, but the bands and the skin over them stay the same. FIT FIRM works on the skin and the layer under it.

How the heat works

The handpiece sends radiofrequency energy into the skin at 448 kHz, and the tissue under the skin warms up. The collagen already there tightens and the warmth signals your skin cells to make new collagen and elastin. New collagen takes weeks to months to form, so your skin keeps getting smoother and firmer after the course ends.

Your session

  1. You talk with the doctor or our Slim specialist team, who look at your shape and the areas you want treated and plan your course.
  2. The skin over the area is cleaned.
  3. A gel goes on the skin to help the energy pass through.
  4. The handpiece is moved over the area with a light massage. It feels warm with a light pull.
  5. After about 20 minutes you can go straight back to your day.

How many sessions

Most people see a change after the first session, a clear change after 4 to 6 and the best result after 8 to 10. How far you get depends on your skin, the number of sessions and how you look after your skin between them.

Who it's for

FIT FIRM is for cellulite and mildly loose skin on the thighs, hips, buttocks, belly, upper arms, inner knees, back or waist, including skin that loosened after pregnancy or weight loss. The front and back of the thighs are the most common areas. Very loose skin after a big weight loss may need surgery to remove, and the doctor will tell you at your consult if FIT FIRM can't give you the result you want.

It can't be used if you're pregnant, have a pacemaker or another electronic implant, have metal implants in the area, or have an infection or open wound on the skin there.

Questions

Does it hurt?

No. It feels warm with a light pull, like a massage.

How is it different from Emsculpt Neo?

FIT FIRM uses heat to smooth cellulite and firm the skin. Emsculpt Neo adds magnetic pulses that work the muscle and is made to remove fat and build muscle.

How many sessions do I need?

The change is usually clear after 4 to 6 sessions and best after 8 to 10. The doctor sets the number after looking at the area.

Is the machine approved?

Yes. The machine is approved by the US FDA and the Thai FDA (อย.).

Can men do it?

Yes.

Can I do it after CoolSculpting?

Yes. FIT FIRM can firm the skin over an area after CoolSculpting has removed some of the fat.
