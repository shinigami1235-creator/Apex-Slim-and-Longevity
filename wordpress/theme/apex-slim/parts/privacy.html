APEX Medical Center Co., Ltd. runs Apex Slim and Longevity and this website. This page says what the website collects about you, why, who sees it, how long it is kept and how to ask for it to be removed, under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What the consult form collects

When you send the Book a consult form, the website keeps:

  • your name
  • your phone number, your LINE ID, or both
  • the goals you ticked and the best time to call you
  • the page you sent the form from
  • the date and time you agreed to be contacted, the consent line you agreed to and the date of this policy
  • a one-way code made from your internet address, which stops one connection sending many requests. The address itself is not kept.

The form does not ask for health details, photos or ID. Please don't type them into it.

Why

The Apex Slim team uses these details to contact you and book your first visit. They are not used for marketing unless you agree to that separately.

Who sees it

Apex Slim staff read requests in the website's admin, and each new request is emailed to the Apex Slim team. The details sit on the website's server and pass through the email service that sends that email. No one else receives them.

How long it is kept

Each request is deleted automatically 365 days after it arrives. Staff can delete it sooner, and do so when you ask.

The Slim Wellness Lab

The body check and the goal photo run in your browser. The numbers you enter and the photo you pick stay on your device and are not sent to Apex or saved.

Cookies

The website needs no cookies to work. If Apex turns on analytics or advertising tools, a banner asks first, and those tools only run after you accept. Your choice is kept in your browser, and Cookie settings at the foot of each page changes it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details the website holds about you, to correct them, to have them deleted, or to withdraw your consent. You can also object to how they are used, and complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee. Apex answers each request within 30 days.
